Acid vs Vana - Winner bracket final
.: DM4 :.

Acid got strong start with RA and he was able to beat Vana down three times before hitting the lava and giving Vana a chance to get the RA. Vana didnt take this gift and got beaten by acid's nice rocket from lower level to quadledge.

After first minute the game was 4-0 to acid. Following minutes were pretty careful playing from both players; vana waited RA and acid flooded teleporter and YA passage. Just when Vana was about to get the control, he got telefragged by Acid at the YA teleporter. Acid thanked and took several easy spawnfrags from Vana and the score suddenly was 11-3 for Acid's favour.

After the spawnfrag-party Vana got some control, but acid had still one ace more: he discharged vana down! After this wacky move acid got bad mega spawn, but when Vana killed Acid at the lower level, Acid got RA spawn and killed easily Vana. Acid controlled the map for few minutes and got a nice 17 to 4 -lead and the game was clear. Acid used his good spawns well and Vana got defeated clearly 26-7.
.: DM2 :.

Start went for Vana, when he got 200/250 straight, but even with that gear he was unable to kill Acid, who was having only 200/100. Between 9 minutes left and 8 minutes left players didnt even see each others, so the game name was to keep you alive until enemy makes a mistake.

First mistake was made by Acid, who got bored with life at hidden ra after a rl-fight against vana - score was 0 - -1. Next happening was just before 5 mins left -sign, when Vana fragged Acid at the hide ra with pineapples. Just before Acid had charged heavily to lower rl, chasing Vana, who was in a low health position. This didnt work though and vana got first good gear again and wisely attacked against Acid: 1- -1.

When having 2 frags lead, vana turned the +back motion on and Acid had to search him all over the level, finally getting a battle contact at 2xya and fragging scary looking Vana: 1-0. This happened after 3 minutes left -sign. Acid's next move was to wait Vana at low rl lift and when Vana came, Acid charged like a maniac towards him - with bad scores. Vana got more hits in and Acid was dead again, 2-0. Right after that both players boosted themselves up and made a nice fight at 2xya, which ended again to Vanas favour, 3-0. Vana decided to visit the lava god just before the round ended but it didnt change the winner. Vana win this dm2 fight with score 2-0.
.: DM6 :.

Acid got the RA spawn and LG straight after. While Vana spawned to GA-side, the first minute was kinda quiet, first fight was seen at middle area but it didnt lead to anyone's death.

After one minute of playing acid made a little trick and forced Vana to bad position at ga->middle passage and Vana bored with life. Vana had a bad luck and Acid got two easy spawnfrags from this happening, too. Following minute didnt bring any news, only one little fight at middle section but it was scoreless. Acid sneaked to RA and Vana charged -> +1 frag for acid.

At the halfway Acid had the game in control and Vana seemed to be unwilling to do any other charging-mistakes and when Acid went to fetch Vana from GA, Vana walked straight to RA and got it. Acid won the battle of RA once again and the score was Acid 5 Vana still -1, when there was less than four minutes to go. When observers thought the game was over, started the Vana-show. He killed Acid in a big fight at megaroom and got one spawnfrag, too. Vana took RA and went to wait for mega, acid followed him and tried to surprise Vana, but it didnt work out too well. Vana killed Acid again in the megaroom and got the mega. Next Vana killed Acid in the middle room and suddenly the game was tight: 5-3. Acid was charging like the game wasnt any official and got killed again, despite his RA - Vana was now only one frag down. Despite the score, Acid seemed to be more keen to find Vana than opposite, but once again Vana showed up at the mega and they had a nice fight again there, scoreless though. Acid went to ra when there was only 1mins to go and vana stayed at ga side.

For some reason Acid gave the second last red armor to Vana, by going to GA side just for no obvious reason. This was a wrong thing to do, because Vana charged heavily there and killed Acid at 15 seconds left mark. Game was tied then and observers were waiting for overtime , but no - more was to come. 4 seconds before the end Vana attacked to RA and met 0/100/rl Acid, and geesh Vana got owned BADLY!. Vana got only one small hit in while Acid made Vana jump up and down with several nice rl shots - a real ownage and the deciding frag. Acid had won this nice dm6 round by 6-5.
