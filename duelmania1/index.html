"First final" of loosers bracket played -- Sunday, June 3 2001, 07:52 pm
Zhin vs Fifi was played today and it ended 2-1 for zhin. Game started with dm4 (zhin´s map) and zhin pretty much controlled the whole round with very nice moves and aim.

Fifi´s map was dm6. Zhin got nice spawns (mh+ra) and controlled few first minutes. After zhin made the first mistake fifi immediately punished for that and took control of the game. Fifi kept control almost till the end so it was 1-1 now, and dm2 as the decider.

DM2 started pretty slowly. Then zhin got the first frag and started playing rather defensively. Fifi managed to kill zhin, but soon died after dipping in the lava. It was 2-1 and fifi was once again chasing zhin. But the game stayed like that to the end. Zhin was really close to get killed in the last few seconds, surviving only with ~30h, but thats plenty. :)

GG
